# Demo providers

These profiles illustrate how the same product can present different service businesses. They are demonstration personas, not paying customers or endorsements. Ratings, reviews, experience claims, client counts, case studies, bookings and amounts shown are illustrative.

## Amelia — career and leadership coaching

A warm, light page for a provider offering online conversations and focused follow-up sessions.

| Example offer | Duration | Illustrative price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery & Coaching Fit | 30 minutes | Free |
| Career Clarity Session | 60 minutes | USD 150 |
| Leadership Growth Session | 60 minutes | USD 175 |
| Decision-Making Intensive | 90 minutes | USD 225 |
| Confidence & Boundaries | 45 minutes | USD 120 |
| Momentum Check-In | 30 minutes | USD 85 |

The page groups offers by need, explains session benefits, introduces the provider and supports FAQs, testimonials and longer case-study content. The booking interface stays alongside the desktop content and adapts to a narrow mobile layout.

## What these examples demonstrate

The product can vary brand treatment, service language, currency and delivery context while retaining a recognisable booking journey. The personas are examples of configuration, not evidence that every industry's operational requirements are supported.

The actual sample pages also contain illustrative ratings and testimonials. Their presence is a UI/content demonstration. No real outcome, professional qualification or Bookvia traction should be inferred from them. [Full gallery](gallery.md).
