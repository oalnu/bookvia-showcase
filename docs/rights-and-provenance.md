# Rights and screenshot provenance

This is a public product showcase for Bookvia. It includes selected application captures and newly written product documentation. It does not include or license the platform's source code.

## Demonstration content

Amelia and Northform Performance are demo providers. Their offers, ratings, testimonials, case studies, client counts, calendar events and amounts are illustrative. They are not evidence of actual clients, revenue, bookings, professional qualifications or business results.

The fresh images were captured from the local development application on 15 October 2026. No replacement UI, generated screenshot content, retouching or compositing was used. Small development-tool indicators can appear in the captures. Captures from earlier development sessions are identified in the gallery and image manifest.

Thirteen fresh captures show the authenticated Amelia workspace with seeded fictional records. The sidebar was collapsed through the application’s own controls to keep the private account email out of images. Client contacts shown use example.com addresses and fictional telephone numbers. The editor’s visible save failure is retained and disclosed. Seeded payment statuses and revenue figures do not represent processed transactions.

Three earlier captures show service onboarding on desktop/mobile and synthetic calendar components. The calendar image is a component verification view, not a new authenticated dashboard acceptance test.

File extensions match the actual image format. Image hashes, dimensions, capture category and dates are listed in [the asset manifest](../assets/manifest.json). No private filesystem paths are included in that manifest.

## Publication boundaries

Application source and history, database exports, configuration, credentials, private account contact details, real client records and private planning/audit files are excluded. Payment and booking identifiers from earlier confirmation captures are excluded too.

Bookvia branding and original showcase material remain protected. Third-party imagery, fonts, logos and service names retain their respective rights. Images of people illustrate demo personas and do not imply that those people are Bookvia customers or endorse the platform. No blanket licence to extract or reuse embedded third-party assets is granted.

AI tools assisted the platform's development and the preparation of this documentation. Product images are application captures, not AI-generated reconstructions of the interface.
